# Colombo Fort Railway Station — reference and fidelity register

Researched 6 September 2026. This is an original architectural interpretation, not a laser scan, a measured survey or a navigation model. Photographs from different periods are combined; the model does not represent a verified date-specific condition.

Official institution: [Sri Lanka Railways](https://www.railway.gov.lk/).

## Interior coverage

The detailed GLB and Blender file include the public ticket hall, counter furniture, queue rails, platform furniture, circulation stairs and roof structure. Staff offices, toilets, shops, service rooms, concealed construction, railway signalling and an exact complete station plan are not claimed. Small decoration and interior collections are excluded from `map.glb`.

All materials and geometry are authored locally. Reference photographs are neither redistributed nor projected onto the building. The included font licence covers the Sinhala outline source. The model's dimensions should be refined against a measured plan or additional on-site photographs before any survey-grade use.
