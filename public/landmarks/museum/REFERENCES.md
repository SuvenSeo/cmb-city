# Colombo National Museum — reference and fidelity register

Researched 6 September 2026. Original geometry based on photographs and published descriptions; not a measured or photogrammetric replica. Reference images span different dates, and the interior is a selective architectural interpretation.

## Interior coverage and uncertainty

The detailed model contains the entry portico, entrance and stair halls, bifurcated staircase, open upper verandas, representative side galleries, timber floors, panelled doors, ceiling mouldings and display furniture. No measured floor plan was available. Wing room divisions, circulation connections, ceiling coffers, service areas and exhibit positions are not verified. Gallery vases are generic display stand-ins, not replicas of museum artifacts. The full collection, conservation rooms, utilities and all later building extensions are not reconstructed. Fine cartouche relief and sculpture are interpreted rather than copied exactly.

Roof collections can be hidden to expose the interior. Exterior, close façade, interior and cutaway cameras are saved in the Blender source. The lightweight city version excludes the interior and smallest decorative geometry.

The photos remain with their respective publishers and are not included in these downloads. No third-party 3D model or photographic texture is embedded. Additional measured drawings and current interior photography would be needed to move beyond a visual architectural reconstruction.
